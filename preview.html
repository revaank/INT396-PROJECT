Understanding India's TB Challenge

🫁

Ancient, Airborne & 100% Curable

TB bacteria have infected humans for over 9,000 years, spreading simply through the air when an infected person speaks or coughs. It is completely curable with antibiotics — yet India carries 27% of the entire world's cases.

⚡ Over 2.4 million Indians are officially notified every year

🔬

The Secret Multipliers

TB rarely attacks alone. Diabetes triples a patient's risk of developing active TB, while Tobacco and Alcohol destroy lung defenses and cause patients to drop out of treatment before being cured.

⚡ 1 in 5 TB patients in India also suffers from diabetes or tobacco dependence
